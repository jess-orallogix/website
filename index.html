Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions

What exactly is OrallogIX?

OrallogIX produces highly standardized and characterized, food-grade allergen extracts derived from whole food sources. Each extract is a characterized protein preparation with a known composition and concentration, produced under controlled conditions and verified by third-party laboratories.

How is your product characterized?

Every production lot for each food extract undergoes SDS-PAGE protein profiling to confirm the expected protein banding pattern as well as an assay to determine protein content. Additionally, each lot is independently validated by accredited third-party laboratories for allergenic component content and to ensure sterility. We provide a full Certificate of Analysis (CoA) with each order.

What concentration are the extracts?

Our food allergen extracts are made to be at a standardized concentration that is approximately double the concentration offered by most alternatives currently on the market. As scientists, we know that this provides greater flexibility in how the product is used.

Which allergens are available?

Starting in mid-2026, we will be offering peanut, milk, egg, cashew, walnut, hazelnut, and sesame extracts. We plan to launch more extracts in late 2026.

Is OrallogIX an FDA-approved drug or biologic?

No. OrallogIX products are food-grade allergen extracts marketed as dietary supplement ingredients. They are not drugs, biologics, or investigational products, and they are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
